01About the company

StockWhisk gives every independent retailer the operational clarity that, until now, only large chains could afford.

StockWhisk is a multi-tenant, cloud-based retail management platform that unifies inventory control, point of sale, purchasing, receivables and payables, warranty and service management, and financial reporting under one securely partitioned roof. Every business operates in its own isolated workspace — its data never mingles with another's.

We are engineering-led, security-first, and obsessive about one number every shopkeeper cares about: true profit. StockWhisk combines accounting-grade correctness with consumer-grade ease of use.

  • What we are: A vertical SaaS platform for retail operations.
  • Who we serve: Single-outlet and multi-branch shops — retail, electronics, mobile & repair, pharmacy, apparel, hardware and more.
  • How we differ: Weighted-average costing, honest COGS, and tenant-grade security are foundational, not afterthoughts.

04Our solution

StockWhisk replaces the notebook, the calculator, and five disconnected apps with one coherent system. Scan an item to sell it — stock decrements and profit is booked against real cost. Receive a purchase order — stock rises and the supplier's due updates. Take a partial payment — the customer's balance and the cash ledger stay in lockstep. Close the month — the profit statement, cash flow and inventory valuation are already computed.

Three principles: truth over vanity (profit after cost, discounts and returns); isolation by design (every shop's data separated); effortless at the counter (fast for a busy till, simple for a new hire).

07Technology stack

  • Backend: Python · Django · Django REST Framework
  • Frontend: Server-rendered templates · Bootstrap · Alpine.js · htmx · Chart.js
  • Database: PostgreSQL with strict tenant scoping
  • Async: Celery + Redis (low-stock alerts, nightly stock reconcile, recurring expenses)
  • APIs: REST with JWT auth, throttling & an enterprise API-key tier
  • Performance: O(1) stock write path, N+1 hotspots removed, cached analytics

08Security & data privacy

  • Tenant isolation — cross-business access architecturally blocked and audited.
  • Role-based access — Owner, Manager, Cashier, Inventory Manager, Accountant.
  • Auth hardening — brute-force lockout (per-IP & per-account), JWT throttling, secure cookies, HSTS.
  • App hardening — Content-Security-Policy, CSRF protection, SQLi-safe ORM, XSS escaping, validated uploads.
  • Data integrity — DB-level non-negative constraints, immutable audit & stock ledgers, atomic concurrency-safe transactions.
